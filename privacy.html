Stateam

Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Stateam is an app for high school athletes and coaches to record sports stats, manage school teams and track progress. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, who can see it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

InformationWhy we collect it
Name, email address, password (stored securely by our sign-in provider), and Google account name if you use Google sign-inTo create your account and let you log in
Account type (player or coach), school, main sport, grade, position and jersey numberTo show you the right tools and connect you with your school’s teams
Personal match and practice logs, ratings and notesTo show your stats, records, trends and achievements
Team information, rosters and official game results and player stats entered by coachesTo share official team records with the team
The date you agreed to this policy and the Terms of ServiceTo keep a record of your consent

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or payment information, and we do not use advertising trackers.

Who can see your information

  • Only you: your personal match and practice logs, notes, and your email address.
  • Signed-in Stateam users: your name, account type, school, grade, main sport, position, jersey number, and the teams you are on, plus official game stats entered by your coach, including team leaderboards.
  • Your team’s coach: the roster, official game stats, and which players have joined using the team’s invite code.
  • The Stateam administrator: the names, schools and email addresses of coach accounts, so coaches can be verified before they manage teams.

We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.

Where your information is stored

Stateam uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to run sign-in and store data. Data is stored on Google Cloud servers in Seoul, South Korea. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own security and privacy terms.

Students under 14

If you are under 14, you need permission from a parent or guardian before creating an account. If we learn that a child under 14 has created an account without that permission, we will delete the account.

How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile and personal logs. Official game stats that list you may stay in your team’s records with your name removed or anonymized.

Your choices and rights

  • You can view and edit your profile in the app at any time.
  • You can delete your personal match and practice logs in the app.
  • You can leave any team in the app.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop processing it, by contacting us.

Security

Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never stored by Stateam. Access to data is controlled by security rules, so for example only a team’s coach can change its official stats and only you can read your personal logs. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don’t use anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will update the date at the top and let you know in the app.
